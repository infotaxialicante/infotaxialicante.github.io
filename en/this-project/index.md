# About this project

**InfoTaxi Alicante** is an information portal focused on the taxi sector in the city of Alicante and its Joint Service Area.

---

## What you will find here

* **Calculator:** Indicative fare estimates for interurban taxi services to help you plan your journeys.

* **Interactive map:** Location and information about taxi ranks in Alicante and its Joint Service Area.

* **Downloads:** Official fares and other official documents.

* **Articles and information:** Publications on regulations, analysis and educational guides.

* **Participation:** A space where users can suggest content and articles of interest, with the taxi at the centre and as a meeting point.

---

## An independent and transparent project

InfoTaxi Alicante is not a booking or dispatch service, a transport company, or an official representative of any association or public authority. It is not intended for commercial purposes either: the website contains no paid advertising, affiliate links, or user tracking or monitoring tools.

All the information provided aims to be as accurate as possible, drawing on official sources and public data, always with respect for the public taxi service and its users.
