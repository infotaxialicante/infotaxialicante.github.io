# Privacy policy

At **InfoTaxi Alicante**, we take the privacy of individuals who use this website seriously. This Privacy policy explains what personal data may be processed when using **infotaxialicante.es**, for what purpose, and what rights belong to the data subjects.

This website is **informational, educational, free of charge, and independent**. It does not use visitors' personal data for behavioral advertising, commercial profiling, or personal analytics.

---

## 1. Data controller

**Data controller:** Bruno Hernández Alcaraz

**Location:** Alicante, Valencian Community (Spain)

**Email:** [contacto@infotaxialicante.es](mailto:contacto@infotaxialicante.es)

**Website:** [https://infotaxialicante.es]({{ '/' | relative_url }})

The data controller is the owner of InfoTaxi Alicante.

The processing of personal data is carried out in accordance with Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (General Data Protection Regulation - GDPR) and Organic Law 3/2018 on the Protection of Personal Data and Guarantee of Digital Rights (LOPDGDD).

---

## 2. Personal data that may be processed

InfoTaxi Alicante does not request personal data for simply browsing its content. Data may be processed when an individual voluntarily communicates with the site or participates in the project.

### Email inquiries

When an individual voluntarily writes to [contacto@infotaxialicante.es](mailto:contacto@infotaxialicante.es), the data provided in the message and the data necessary to respond to the inquiry—such as name, email address, and the content of the communication—may be processed.

### Submissions and article proposals

When an individual participates through the procedure established on the [Get involved]({{ 'en/get-involved/' | relative_url }}) page, the data provided via the LiberaForms form and subsequent email communications may be processed.

This data may include:

* First and last name;
* Email address;
* Preferred alias for publication, if provided;
* Declarations regarding legal age;
* Declarations regarding authorship and rights to the article;
* Declarations regarding rights or authorization to use an image, where applicable;
* Information necessary to manage the proposal and related communications.

The article and, if applicable, the proposed image **are not submitted via the form**, but subsequently by email, following the procedure indicated on the [Get involved]({{ 'en/get-involved/' | relative_url }}) page.

---

## 3. Purposes of processing

Personal data may be processed for the following purposes:

* Attending to and answering inquiries received by email;
* Managing participation requests;
* Receiving, evaluating, and processing article proposals;
* Maintaining necessary communications related to such proposals;
* Verifying declarations made by participants;
* Managing, where applicable, authorizations to publish content;
* Managing actions related to the publication or removal of content;
* Handling requests to exercise rights recognized under data protection regulations;
* Complying with applicable legal obligations.

Data will not be used to create commercial profiles, display behavioral advertising, or send unsolicited commercial communications.

---

## 4. Legal basis for processing

The legal basis for processing depends on the purpose for which the data was provided.

In the case of the LiberaForms participation form, the legal basis is the **consent** of the data subject, expressed through an affirmative action by filling out and submitting the form.

Consent may be withdrawn at any time. The withdrawal of consent shall not affect the lawfulness of processing based on consent before its withdrawal.

In the case of communications initiated voluntarily by email, data will be processed to manage and respond to the communication received and, where appropriate, to address actions requested by the data subject.

When necessary, certain processing activities may be carried out to comply with legal obligations applicable to the controller.

---

## 5. Recipients and services used

InfoTaxi Alicante does not sell, rent, or transfer personal data for commercial purposes, nor does it use visitors' personal data for behavioral advertising.

To provide certain technical services, external providers related to the operation of the website and its tools may be involved.

### GitHub Pages

The website is hosted using **GitHub Pages**. As part of its technical service provision and infrastructure security measures, GitHub may process certain technical connection data from visitors.

InfoTaxi Alicante does not request or use visitors' IP addresses to identify individuals, create profiles, display behavioral advertising, or perform personal analytics.

### DonDominio

**DonDominio** provides services related to the domain and email accounts used by InfoTaxi Alicante. Data processed through these services is also subject to the terms and privacy policies of this provider.

### LiberaForms

The form used to manage participations is provided by **LiberaForms**.

Data entered into the form is stored on the infrastructure of this service so that it can be managed by InfoTaxi Alicante.

Processing carried out through LiberaForms is also subject to its own privacy policy and terms of service.

### OpenStreetMap

The taxi rank page uses maps based on **OpenStreetMap**. To display the maps, the visitor's browser sends requests to the servers that provide cartographic data and maps.

These requests may involve the processing of certain technical connection data by the services used.

### Leaflet and unpkg

Map visualization relies on the **Leaflet** library, whose assets are loaded from **unpkg**, an external asset distribution service.

Loading these resources involves technical requests from the visitor's browser to the corresponding service.

### Cloudflare

**Cloudflare** is used exclusively to handle the domain redirection from **infotaxialicante.com** to **infotaxialicante.es**.

Cloudflare is not used as an analytics, behavioral advertising, or visitor tracking service for the .es version of the website.

### Google Maps

Some pages may include external links to **Google Maps** to facilitate navigation to specific locations.

These links do not embed maps via iframe nor do they use a Google Maps API within the website. By clicking on one of these links, the visitor accesses an external service subject to its own terms and privacy policy.

## 7. Cookies and tracking technologies

InfoTaxi Alicante does not use first-party cookies intended for personal analytics, behavioral advertising, or profiling.

The site's tools and content have been configured, as far as possible, to avoid unnecessary tracking mechanisms.

However, certain external services used for the operation of the website may receive requests from the visitor's browser and process technical data necessary to deliver their services, according to their own policies and terms.

---

## 8. Data retention

Personal data will be retained for as long as necessary to manage the purpose for which it was provided.

In the case of participation proposals that are not published, data will be retained only for the time necessary to evaluate and close the proposal and manage related communications. Once this process is completed and data is no longer needed, it will be deleted.

When a proposal is published, data necessary to prove authorship, publication authorization, and handle potential requests related to the content will be retained for as long as the content remains published.

If the author requests the removal of published content, it will be removed, and personal data that is no longer necessary will be deleted, except for data that must be retained due to a legal obligation or as required for the establishment, exercise, or defense of legal claims.

Data managed through external services may also be subject to the retention periods and technical conditions of those services.

When data is no longer necessary for the relevant purposes, it will be deleted or, where a legal obligation to retain exists, stored for the corresponding statutory period.

---

## 9. External services and their privacy policies

Certain technical elements of the website are provided by external services. These services may process certain technical data when the visitor's browser connects to their servers.

Each provider applies its own terms and privacy policies.

Services used include:

* GitHub Pages, for website hosting.
* DonDominio, for domain and email services.
* LiberaForms, for participation form management.
* OpenStreetMap, for cartographic data and maps.
* unpkg, for Leaflet asset distribution.
* Cloudflare, exclusively for redirecting the .com domain to the .es domain.

---

## 10. Email communications

Communications voluntarily sent to InfoTaxi Alicante contact addresses may contain personal data.

This data will be processed solely to manage the corresponding communication and, where applicable, to address requests made by the data subject.

Email addresses will not be used to send advertising or unsolicited commercial communications.

In the case of participations, email communications may form part of managing the proposal, including confirming the final version of content prior to publication.

---

## 11. No commercialization of data

InfoTaxi Alicante does not sell, rent, or commercialize personal data.

Data voluntarily provided by individuals who contact or participate is not used to create commercial databases, run advertising campaigns, or build profiles for commercial purposes.

---

## 12. Rights of data subjects

Individuals whose personal data is processed may, where applicable, exercise the rights recognized under data protection laws, including:

* Access;
* Rectification;
* Erasure (Right to be forgotten);
* Restriction of processing;
* Objection;
* Data portability;
* Withdrawal of consent when processing is based on it.

To exercise these rights, a request may be sent to [contacto@infotaxialicante.es](mailto:contacto@infotaxialicante.es), specifying the right to be exercised and providing the necessary information to process the request.

Exercising these rights is free of charge, except in cases provided for by applicable regulations.

Requests will be addressed within statutory time limits.

---

## 13. Right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority

If an individual considers that the processing of their personal data does not comply with applicable regulations, they may lodge a complaint with the [Spanish Data Protection Agency - AEPD](https://www.aepd.es), without prejudice to any other administrative or judicial remedy that may apply.

---

## 14. Removal of published content

The removal of an article or other material published on InfoTaxi Alicante is a matter independent from the exercise of data protection rights.

The author of a contribution may request removal via [colaboraciones@infotaxialicante.es](mailto:colaboraciones@infotaxialicante.es), in accordance with the procedure established on the [Get involved]({{ 'en/get-involved/' | relative_url }}) page.

Removal does not necessarily imply the immediate deletion of all data related to the contribution when a legal obligation to retain exists or when certain data is required for the establishment, exercise, or defense of legal claims.

---

## 15. Security

InfoTaxi Alicante adopts reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal data against unauthorized access, loss, alteration, or improper processing.

However, no electronic transmission or storage system can guarantee absolute security.

This privacy policy may be updated when necessary to reflect changes in the operation of InfoTaxi Alicante, in the services used, or in applicable regulations.
