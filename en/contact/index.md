## Important information

> **Note regarding taxi service:**
> **InfoTaxi Alicante is strictly an informational and educational portal.** Taxi bookings cannot be made through this website, nor do we handle vehicle requests or provide transport services.
> If you need to request a taxi immediately or make a booking, please contact the official RadioTeleTaxi Alicante channels directly or go to the nearest taxi rank.

**RadioTeleTaxi Alicante request channels:**

* **WhatsApp:** [965 25 25 11](https://wa.me/34965252511)
* **Phone:** [965 25 25 11](tel:+34965252511)
