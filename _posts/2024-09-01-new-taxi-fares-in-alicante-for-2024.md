The Alicante City Council has approved the **new taxi fares** that will come into effect next month. This update represents an average increase of **3.5%** compared to the previous year.

## 📊 Official New Fares

### Fare 1 (Daytime)
**Schedule:** From 07:00 to 21:00 hours

| Concept | Price |
|----------|--------|
| Flag drop (Bajada de bandera) | €1.75 |
| Price per kilometer | €0.95 |
| Price per hour of waiting time | €18.50 |

### Fare 2 (Nighttime & Holidays)
**Schedule:** From 21:00 to 07:00 hours

| Concept | Price |
|----------|--------|
| Flag drop (Bajada de bandera) | €2.10 |
| Price per kilometer | €1.10 |
| Price per hour of waiting time | €20.50 |

> **Important:** Surcharges for luggage, pets, or airport service remain unchanged.

## 📅 Key Dates

- **September 15**: Publication in the Official Gazette
- **October 1**: Entry into force of the new fares

## 🧮 How much will my trip cost?

You can calculate the exact cost of your trip using our [fare calculator](/en/calculator/).

### Indicative price examples:

- **Airport → City Center**: €18-22
- **Train Station → Plaza de Toros**: €7-9
- **San Juan Beach → City Hall**: €12-15
- **City Center → University**: €10-13

## 📋 What happens to current fares?

Current fares will remain valid until **September 30**. Starting October 1, all taxis must apply the new rates.

## 🔍 More Information

For further details, check the official [council document](/assets/docs/tarifas-taxi-2024.pdf) or visit the [Mobility Institute](https://www.alicante.es/movilidad) website.
